Where to look

The main open textbook collections and what you'll find in each. All are free. Check each book's license before you plan to remix it.

Ready-made textbooks

Start here if you want to adopt a complete book.

OpenStax

AdoptRemixCC BY-NC-SA 4.0

Peer-reviewed textbooks for high-enrollment intro courses, from Rice University. Free online and as PDFs, with low-cost print copies. Every book uses CC BY-NC-SA 4.0, so remixes must stay noncommercial and carry the same license. OpenStax also offers Assignable, a course tool that pairs its books with assignments.

Open Textbook Library

AdoptRemixFaculty reviews

About 1,900 complete textbooks, run by the University of Minnesota's Center for Open Education. Every book must be able to support a full semester course, and books with no-changes (ND) licenses have been phased out. About 70% of the books have reviews written by faculty, which makes this the best place to judge quality before adopting.

Noba

PsychologyBuild your ownCC BY-NC-SA 4.0

More than 105 psychology modules written by experts. You can adopt a ready-made collection (for example, Developmental Psychology) or pick modules to build a custom text. Many modules come with instructor manuals, slides, and test banks. Noba uses the same license as OpenStax, so the two can be combined into one book.

Remixing platforms

Start here if you want to assemble a book from several sources.

LibreTexts

RemixHomework systemLicense varies by page

More than 3,000 textbooks across 17 subject libraries, with a built-in tool for combining chapters from different books with your own material. ADAPT, its free homework system, has more than 300,000 openly licensed questions. Licenses can differ from page to page, even within one book, so check each chapter you use.

OER Commons

SearchAuthoring tool

A digital library of open materials run by ISKME, an education nonprofit. Besides textbooks, it holds lessons, modules, and activities. Its Open Author tool lets you create, license, and share your own materials.

Search engines

Use these when the collections above don't have what you need.

OASIS

Search100+ sources

A search tool from SUNY Geneseo's library that searches more than 100 open content sources at once, including textbooks, courses, videos, and modules. It's the fastest way to see everything that exists for a course.

MERLOT

SearchPeer reviews

More than 110,000 free online learning materials, with peer reviews by faculty for some items. Not everything here is openly licensed. Some items are free to use but can't be copied or changed.
